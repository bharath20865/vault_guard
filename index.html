Your privacy comes first

Vault Guard checks passwords locally in this browser. It does not send your password to a server or save it. The strength score is an educational estimate, not a guarantee that a password has never been exposed in a data breach.

Good habits: use a unique password for every account, prefer a password manager, turn on multi-factor authentication, and never share your passwords.
